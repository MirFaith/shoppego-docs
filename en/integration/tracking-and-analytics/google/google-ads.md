# Google Ads

To set up Google Ads integration with Shoppego account, you need to get Google Ads ID, **Purchase label**, **Begin checkout label** and **Add to cart label**.

You need to make sure you already have your own Google Ads account for you to get the required keys.

You can refer to the link below for steps to get the required keys.

Also, make sure you have **Google Analytics** settings for your Shoppego store to enable you to create these **Google Ads settings**. If you have not yet set up Google Analytics for your store, click on the button below:

{% hint style="info" %}
For "forms", you can still follow this tutorial. However, you need to choose the Leads Label ID.
{% endhint %}

## Log in to your Google Ads account

You can **log in** to your **Google Ads account** first to make these settings. You can click on this link to log in: <https://ads.google.com/home/>

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/LBr1BeHUPHbeAdTxKc3J.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Setup in Shoppego

Once you get all the keys you need, you can link your Google Ads with Shoppego by entering all the keys in your Shoppego account.

For more information, you can refer to the setup steps below:

1\. Log in to your Shoppego account. Click on **Settings > General.** Next, you can scroll down to enable the **Enable Google Ads Conversion Tracking** toggle

![](../../../../.gitbook/assets/ACoyKky4NYBvxn2od33u.jpg)

5\. Then, you can **enter all the keys in the space provided**

![](../../../../.gitbook/assets/wuHVmuqZ7f0pMODgQb2y.jpg)

6\. Once done , click on **Save**.

![](../../../../.gitbook/assets/APjbrnJWxpfqDdEYI2Ky.jpg)
