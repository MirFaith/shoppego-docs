# Bump Offers

**Bump Offers** is a function of Shoppego under marketing to increase the conversion rate of your online store. In simple language, it is to be **cross-sold** to buyers.

{% hint style="success" %}
These features are only available for <mark style="color:red;">**Premium**</mark> and <mark style="color:red;">**Ultimate**</mark>**&#x20;plan** only.
{% endhint %}

The order bump will be **displayed on the Checkout section** which is when the buyer fills in the information for delivery.

## Setup Bump Offer

1. Log in to Shoppego dashboard click on the **Marketings** -> **Bump Offers**

![](../../.gitbook/assets/ItpmwBG9Fo2ec6YDcsCM.jpg)

2. Click on the **Create bump offer** button.

<table><thead><tr><th width="177">Field</th><th>Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Name</strong></td><td>Name of the bump you want to create.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Title</strong></td><td><strong>Headline</strong> that can attract the buyer's attention.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Products Offer</strong></td><td>Place the product to upsell in this section.</td></tr></tbody></table>

3. You can **place as many products** in this section and they will be **displayed randomly** to your buyers.
4. Next, click the **Save** button

![](../../.gitbook/assets/-Mine1Fnl1oapgKnJ9zE.jpg)

The process of creating a Bump offer is complete. Good luck!
