# Preferences

## Homepage Title&#x20;

1. To change the Homepage Title name, you can go to:&#x20;

**Dashboard Overview -> Online Stores -> Preferences -> Homepage Title.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/oai5bBMV6AhgvcGWywHc.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. After making the changes, make sure to click the **Save** button and check if the changes have taken effect on your website. You can refer to the image below.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cpLQEY2mTTAYxM3052WY.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/BbPIgCp376llbMIb4410.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
