# Trial Plan

If you are interested in trying Shoppego before becoming an active member of us, you can [register for free by trying 30 days Free Trial](https://my.shoppego.com/signup) from us.

**Your Free Trial Account** will start after your first registration in Shoppego. During this 30 day period, you can do store set up on your e-commerce store.

### After the expiration of the 30 day Free Trial

### During the 30 day Free Trial

* With the free plan, you **can only set up your store** and you **able to process transactions** but to set up a <mark style="color:red;">**custom domain for your ecommerce website is forbidden**</mark>.&#x20;

### After the expiration of the 30 day Free Trial

* You can choose whether to continue the subscription or not.&#x20;
* You can **upgrade your account** by selecting any of the [Shoppego Subscription Packages](https://shoppego.com/pricing).
* Your account <mark style="color:red;">**is not deleted**</mark> but you <mark style="color:red;">**cannot make any changes**</mark> or <mark style="color:red;">**additions**</mark> to your website.&#x20;
* Your website will also <mark style="color:red;">**not be live**</mark> and <mark style="color:red;">**cannot be viewed**</mark> by anyone.
