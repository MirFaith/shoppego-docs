# Digital Product

Digital products are products that do not have a physical form such as E-books and online courses.

To add your product to the Shoppego system, you can upload your product first.

## Part 1 : Upload Product <a href="digital-product.md#masukkan-produk" id="masukkan-produk"></a>

1\. Log in to your Shoppego account -> Click on **Products** -> **Add product**

![](../../.gitbook/assets/JVCockxjBTFJq4UGqL3z.png)

2\. You will be taken to the product pages. Here you need to enter your product details such as:

## Part 2 : Inventory

### 1 : View Inventory Logs

{% hint style="info" %}
\*\*<mark style="color:red;">**Note**</mark>: The **SKU section** is for **monitoring data for your product** and <mark style="color:red;">**not**</mark>**&#x20;for updating stock.**
{% endhint %}

In this section, you can view changes or track the quantity made in the product stock section.

1. Click on view inventory logs.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ZhWoo5AbRIo4a8qW30Kx.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. In this section, all changes made to the product stock quantity will be recorded.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/6BbhvSW3EFgyg9090m10.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<table><thead><tr><th width="202">Field</th><th>Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Address</strong></td><td><strong>You can change the address in this section to view the logs based on location.</strong></td></tr><tr><td><strong>Date</strong></td><td><strong>Each change made will have a date recorded.</strong></td></tr><tr><td><strong>Activity</strong></td><td><strong>Refers to the thing you did when changing stock quantity.</strong></td></tr><tr><td><strong>Adjusted by</strong></td><td><strong>Refers to the person who made the change to the stock.</strong></td></tr><tr><td><strong>Available</strong></td><td><strong>Referring to the latest stock value.</strong></td></tr></tbody></table>

### 2 : Product Stock

1. To **add product stock**, you need to enter it in the space provided. You only need to **click on the dropdown.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/wdOygixkmnD7w13SxKh8.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. After that, you can **select the reason or information** related to the change in your product stock.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Yu8F9hTI0aZtbWbDX3aQ.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. You will see the latest updated value for your product stock.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
If the **Manage Stock button is&#x20;**<mark style="color:red;">**not**</mark>**&#x20;turned on**, your product will be considered to have <mark style="color:blue;">**unlimited**</mark>**&#x20;stock**.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/FCPnWMw1Hexr5r6teGsR.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. If you press the **View Inventory Logs button**, you will see a **list of information** regarding the **changes to your product stock.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/U4SfKlNeluUWCmDESHTU.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Part 3 : Product Image <a href="digital-product.md#masukkan-produk-image" id="masukkan-produk-image"></a>

Once you have entered your product details, you can upload a picture for that product.&#x20;

{% hint style="success" %}
The **supported image file extensions** are **jpg, jpeg, png, gif,** and **webp** only.
{% endhint %}

1\. To insert a picture you can click on **Images.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/LskFmnVlJWeWlPCOYuHa.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2\. Next, click on **Add image** and upload the image you want. You can upload as many pictures as you want.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
(**Best size for product**: 1000px X 1000px | **Best image size**: Under 200kb)
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/q2HKmMqjJx8LegeL7ObV.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3\. Once you have successfully uploaded your product image, the Uploaded popup will appear on the screen.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
If you want **to arrange the product picture you can drag the arrow button** and arrange the position of your product picture.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/acNkocpuZCX72Rg2HCUV.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Part 4: Adding Files for Digital Products

1\. To enter files for your buyers to download your digital product, you can click on **Files.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Xmy7J6NPaSyKLPnN9pyZ.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2\. Next you can insert the files URL by clicking on **Add file**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/xfBkSLBMGiOwpK1h1emv.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3\. Later, the display will appear as follows. You need to enter the details of the file:

* **File name:** File Name
* **File URL:** Link files for your Digital Product.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ksdxmvtuv4RwOlpJ1R87.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Part 5 : Digital Product Variants

You can set up file variants for your digital products. If you set it to:

* **All Variants**: Your customers will **receive all files** placed in the Files section.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/pJRPGaso4KXHJm8hxid6.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Specific Variant** : Files will be given to the **selected variants.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/8YSTv6quLGFXnFZRKuru.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
