# Captcha Providers

**Integration with this captcha provider&#x20;**<mark style="color:red;">**must be set up**</mark>**&#x20;for each user&#x20;**<mark style="color:red;">**to avoid the risk of their store being spammed by any bot or other attack**</mark>**.**

At Shoppego, you can add a captcha to your website. Among the captchas that you can add to the website is Google reCAPTCHA V3, hCaptcha and Cloudflare Turnstile.
