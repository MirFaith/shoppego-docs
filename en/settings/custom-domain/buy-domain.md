# Buy Domain

At Shoppego, you can now purchase domains directly from the Shoppego dashboard. For every domain purchased via the dashboard, there is no need to configure DNS settings, as the system handles this automatically. You can also manage your DNS record settings directly through the Shoppego dashboard.

## On this page:

* Purchase a domain
* Domain management

## Purchase A Domain

1. Log in to the Shoppego dashboard

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on **Settings**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-click-settings.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on **Custom domain**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-settings-click-custom-domain.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click on "**Buy new domain**"

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-custom-domain-click-buy-new-domain.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. **Search for your desired domain** and click "**Buy**" to proceed with the payment. **You will then be asked to check your email to complete the verification and other necessary steps.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-custom-domain-buy-new-domain.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-buy-domain-forms.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-custom-domain-payment-success-for-new-domain.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Your domain purchase is now complete.

## Domain Management

On the "**Custom domain**" page, you can proceed to manage the domain you have just purchased.

You can click the "**Manage**" button for the domain you just purchased, and you will then be able to view the settings:

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-custom-domain-click-manage.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Set As Redirect

You can use this setting if you wish to redirect any user attempting to access your domain URL to your website URL. For instance, if you have only one domain configuration, enabling this function will redirect any user trying to access that domain to your .myshoppegram.com subdomain URL.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-manage-domain-click-set-as-redirect.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Auto Renew

You can toggle this switch if you want to ensure your domain subscription payment is processed automatically before the expiration date, or vice versa.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-manage-domain-click-auto-renew.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Add custom record

If you wish to add your own custom DNS records, you can click the "**Add custom record**" button and select the record you want to add.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-manage-domain-click-add-custom-record.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Remove DNS Record

If you wish to remove any unwanted DNS records, you can click the **trash can icon** **next to the specific records you want to delete**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-manage-domain-click-remove-dns.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Remove Domain

If you no longer wish to use this domain for your Shoppego store setup, you can click the "**More**" > "**Remove domain**" button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-manage-domain-click-remove-domain.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
