# Email marketing providers

This email marketing provider integration function allows you to bring your customer data from the Shoppego system to the available email marketing provider system.

The customer data that will be brought is only new customer data and it can be created automatically through the customer's own payment at Checkout or you can create the customer data yourself in the Customers dashboard.

If the customer already has an account in your Shoppego account, their data will not be brought into the Email marketing provider's system that you have connected.

For information, the function is only available on Premium and Ultimate plans only.
