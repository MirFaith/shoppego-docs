## Settings that need to be configured

* Ensure you already have a Geoapify account.
* [Make sure you have set the shipping rates by distance](../shipping.md#setting-up-distance-based-rates)
* [Ensure your store location information has been set up correctly.](../locations.md)
* Get the API key from your Geoapify account.
* Do the setting in the Shoppego dashboard

## Settings on Geoapify

If you do not yet have a Geoapify account, you can sign up at this link:<https://myprojects.geoapify.com/register>

1. Log in to your Geoapify account

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/qEmkJKi51iBk6LjcFOky.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on the **Create a new project/Add a new project** button

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/KhuS6JPSlkf22s5sJE06.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. **Enter your project details** to serve as a future reference.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/GcPzRGoRQcTnnSebSbmJ.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. You can **copy the provided API key**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/4ynPaOAJUlbso3DanQZU.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Settings on Shoppego

1. Log in to your Shoppego account

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/lCN7aw3FznaFFoOAyya2.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on **Settings**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/VsK9zs6aZtUfKK1hcCiM.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on **Geolocation providers**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/I8x6cusKRcPpDilHFPdC.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click **Activate/Edit on Geoapify**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/WW1IB7iTCOr8YOJMW75Z.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. **Enter the API key you just copied from your Geoapify account** and click **Save**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/kZNUc18c8K4LujB9XGwj.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
