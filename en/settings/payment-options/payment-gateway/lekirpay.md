# Lekirpay

## Lekirpay Registration Account

Click on this link [http://app.lekirpay.com/validate?referralid=RL000316](<http://app.lekirpay.com/validate?referralid=RL000316
>) to register your Lekirpay account.&#x20;

{% hint style="success" %}
For Lekirpay account registration process, you will be required to :&#x20;

* **Have your own SSM**.
* **Current Bank Account**.
  {% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Account verification, fees, and transactional funds,** they are **fully managed by Lekirpay.**
{% endhint %}

## Getting all required Key from Lekirpay Account

To connect Lekirpay with Shoppego website, you will be required to obtain several keys for verification process. The keys that will be required are **ID**, **Lekirpay** and **Lekir-signature Token**.

To start setup Lekir Payment Gateway, kindly follow this guide :&#x20;

1. Log in into your Lekirpay Account

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/HQM21KdVz8VF5Oh1ysGd.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2\. Next, on the Lekirpay Dashboard, click on Tab **Account**

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/EuHSLSzDWtFUbdDgCxjY.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3\. Then, you will see your API , click on the text **API**, you will see there a three crucial keys will be required later which are **ID**, **Lekirpay** and **Lekir-signature key**.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/OxlomCnpMQx9L5RKrLVQ.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will be required to save the three keys for next steps. Keys you need to save up are :

* **ID**
* **Lekirkey**
* **Lekir-signature Key**

## **Shoppego Setup**

To setup payment gateway **Lekirpay** in Shoppego, you need to activate the payment gateway and insert the **three keys** in your payment gateway setting.

1. Log in into your Shoppego account.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/lCN7aw3FznaFFoOAyya2.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2\. Next, click on **Settings** tab.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/VsK9zs6aZtUfKK1hcCiM.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3\. Then, on **Settings** page, click on **Payment Options** button.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/CA5qmbxGnMXdjUXh5W4M.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4\. On **Payment Options** page, click on **Activate/Edit** button on **Lekirpay** payment gateway tab.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Qr9sa7S7ZtafjmNQjDEU.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5\. You will be redirected to **Lekirpay** payment gateway setup page.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/jpiWPih4rK1gfHnpP7JD.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6\. You will need to key in three keys which are **ID**, **Lekirpay** and **Lekir-signature Key**.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/uLtAJb8kHAEaxSREEpAy.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7\. After that, you can click **Save** to save the setting.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
If you want to perform a **real transaction**, kindly <mark style="color:red;">**deactivate**</mark> the **Test Mode** toggle
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/z2CZ1p4VEDWkkksJdpe5.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
