# Tax Rates

At Shoppego, you can now add tax charges to your customers for each of their purchases.

## Create Tax Rate

To do this setting, you can go to the section:

1. Log in to your Shoppego account

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/lCN7aw3FznaFFoOAyya2.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on **Settings**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/VsK9zs6aZtUfKK1hcCiM.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on **Tax rates**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/76e7DOMRd4psnJVc81MA.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click on the **Add tax rate** button.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/d8XgKRi0bRvCkvQbTXHD.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Next, you can make settings for your tax information.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/qjxxEjth949fmgiq58cq.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

| Parameter    | Description                                                                                                  |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Country      | You can choose the country you want to charge this tax to.                                                   |
| Tax name     | You can enter the name for the tax you are charging. For example SST, GST, etc.                              |
| Tax rate (%) | You can enter the tax percentage you will charge.                                                            |
| Tax type     | You can choose to charge this tax based on the total purchase price of products only or products + shipping. |
| Enabled      | You can tick (Enabled) or untick (Disabled) this toggle to activate or deactivate this tax.                  |

6. Once finished, click **Save**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/0nopTimDEuatNZlP9414.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Next, you need to set the tax on the product you want to tax.**

## Taxation on products

1. Log in to your Shoppego account

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/lCN7aw3FznaFFoOAyya2.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on **Products**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/3QYpKlTI5mdyrdLeiYts.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on the product you want to tax

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/rGpapV4QmAslmyzaG71Y.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click on the **Variants** tab.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IlvCLWZTHhlTmPX1MtvS.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Next, you need to click on the **variant selection** that you want to charge the tax on.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/TcIivurXaYTU56Ch8N3B.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Tick ​​on the toggle **Charge tax on this variant**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/GeLBn3YxWD86S0kpvRFy.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Once finished, click **Save**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/YNhr0Co7BDawG1lfMKoe.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Then, you can repeat** [**this step**](tax-rates.md#taxation-on-products) **to select other variants and products that you want to charge tax on.**
