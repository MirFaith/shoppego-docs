# Settings

To modify your Shopee Seller integration settings, whether for location or the Shopee Seller account, you can go to the following section:

1. Log in to your Shoppego account

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/lCN7aw3FznaFFoOAyya2.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on **Sales Channels**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ukUIKKXQHEeJSqpbtcqi.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on **Shopee**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/k14o4brp5OcdaxHweyJS.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click on **Settings**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/v16i9OTK0o2k6reJZEYv.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

On this page, you will find several settings and pieces of information, including:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ZhfurudTB2bUpJM6kwCZ.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### View Shopee account integration info

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2XtJ2ClEgwl1QYnpyJ5x.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Perform a location change to reconcile stock.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/OYkxz0yz1rOLalY8b2Xw.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Deactivate Shopee integration

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/OOv14PMDr7oj8dLKyOmw.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Remove Shopee account integration

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/7jHVSVX0grsetYSvZ4yy.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
