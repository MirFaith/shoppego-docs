# Settings

To change the TikTok Shop integration settings, whether it's location settings or TikTok Shop accounts, you can go to the section:

1. Log in to your Shoppego account

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/lCN7aw3FznaFFoOAyya2.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on **Sales Channels**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ukUIKKXQHEeJSqpbtcqi.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on **TikTok Shop**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2L0MsuTzNMaUEFPDLp2O.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click on **Settings**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/MANbmatad3DpWrSK64E5.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

On this page, there are several settings and information that you can see, including:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/7IwuBcr95U5qptAid1WJ.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### See TikTok Shop account integration info

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/DJGLDUXkeUqyySPPFJaw.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Make location changes to align stock

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/WCfyb61jBxve5plwM0PW.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Deactivate the TikTok Shop integration

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/4kKLM9d6urpMNth8SOt3.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Removing TikTok Shop account integration

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/cRSK2RY7fdZAei3K8iSI.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
