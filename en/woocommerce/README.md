# Woocommerce

WooCommerce is a free, open-source e-commerce plugin designed to transform any standard WordPress site into a fully functional online store. You can use WooCommerce integration features to synchronize your product data with WooCommerce. **Please note that the system will only synchronize product stock for**:

* **Stock changes from the Shoppego platform**
* **Stock changes through purchases on the Shoppego platform**
* **Stock changes via purchases on the WooCommerce platform.**

**The stock synchronization function between the Shoppego and WooCommerce platforms will also be guided by the SKU settings of the products or product variants themselves.**

{% hint style="danger" %}
This feature is available only for the **Ultimate** plan subscription.
{% endhint %}

## WordPress account registration

If you do not yet have a WordPress account for the WooCommerce plugin setup process, you can sign up via this link first: [https://wordpress.com/setup/onboarding/user?start\_ref=https%3A%2F%2Fwordpress.com%2F](https://wordpress.com/setup/onboarding/user?start_ref=https%3A%2F%2Fwordpress.com%2F)

## Product Format For Woocommerce

Once you have logged into your WordPress account dashboard, you can add products to WooCommerce as usual.

You need to ensure that each of your variants has a unique SKU for the synchronization process with your Shoppego store later.

## Product Format for Shoppego

You need to ensure that every product on Shoppego for which you wish to synchronize stock with WooCommerce has the same SKU as the corresponding product on your WooCommerce platform.

To set up SKUs on the Shoppego platform, you can go to the following section:

1. Log in to your Shoppego account

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/lCN7aw3FznaFFoOAyya2.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on **Products**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/3QYpKlTI5mdyrdLeiYts.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on the product for which you want to adjust the stock, or you can simply create a new product.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/WsykI0oqPDfVUycKw15s.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. You can go to the **Variants** tab.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/hcJh169rZEzDyT4AnyPO.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Click on the variant selection tab where you wish to add SKU settings to synchronize stock.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/DhqDZd1pxafo33ZP23v1.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Then, you can **enter the same SKU designation as your Woocommerce product on the "SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)" designation on the Shoppego platform** and click **Save**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/GWKksQRhlZDjve4OPIXq.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Woocommerce setup on Shoppego

1. Log in to your Shoppego account

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/lCN7aw3FznaFFoOAyya2.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on **Sales channels**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ukUIKKXQHEeJSqpbtcqi.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on "**Manage sites**" in **WooCommerce**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-sales-channels-click-woocommerce.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Next, you can enter your WooCommerce website URL/link into the "**Site address**" setting field and click "**Connect site**".

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-woocommerce-click-connect-site.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will subsequently be asked to "**Approve**" the integration settings. If you have not yet logged into your Woocommerce account, you may be prompted to log in first.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/shoppego-x-woocommerce-approve.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Your Shoppego store is now connected to your WooCommerce account.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-woocommerce-connected.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
