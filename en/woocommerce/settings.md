# Settings

To modify your WooCommerce integration settings, whether for location or the WooCommerce account, you can go to the following section:

1. Log in to your Shoppego account

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/lCN7aw3FznaFFoOAyya2.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click on **Sales Channels**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/ukUIKKXQHEeJSqpbtcqi.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on **WooCommerce**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-click-woocommerce.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click on Settings

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-woocommerce-click-settings.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

On this page, you will find several settings and pieces of information, including:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/dashboard-shoppego-woocommerce-settings.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
